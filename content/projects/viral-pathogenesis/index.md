We investigate how coronavirus infection damages cells and tissues, and how host responses influence disease severity. Comparing highly pathogenic and seasonal coronaviruses helps reveal the mechanisms behind their different outcomes.

The group also studies the determinants of variant transmissibility, pathogenicity and cross-species transmission.
