Emerging viruses rely on host cells to enter, replicate and spread. We study the host and viral factors that govern these steps, with an emphasis on highly pathogenic coronaviruses.

Our questions include which cellular factors coronaviruses use for entry and replication, and why some coronaviruses transmit more efficiently than others.
