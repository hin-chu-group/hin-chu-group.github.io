Mechanistic virology can point to new ways of stopping infection. Recent collaborative work involving Hin Chu has explored broad-spectrum inhibitors of coronaviruses, including compounds that target host entry pathways.

Examples include the 2025 TMP1 bispecific inhibitor study and the 2026 MB-32 small-molecule antiviral study.
